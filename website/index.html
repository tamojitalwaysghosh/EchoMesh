The Story Behind EchoMesh

Most of us only notice how internet-dependent communication is when the internet suddenly disappears. During a trip to Sikkim, this became very real.

At first, disconnecting from the network felt peaceful. But one question stayed: what about people who actually live there, or hikers, rescue teams, and travelers moving through no-signal zones?

It felt strange that two people can stand close to each other, yet their phones still depend on a far-away cellular tower to exchange a message.

"Why can't nearby phones just talk to each other directly?"

How EchoMesh Works

Bluetooth Only

No internet, no SIM, and no Wi-Fi needed for nearby communication.

Dual Device Role

Each phone acts as both a scanner and a host node in the network.

MVP Today

Direct peer messaging over BLE using GATT characteristics and JSON payloads.

Mesh Vision

Future message-hop routing: A -> B -> C when direct reach is not possible.

Current Stage

This is a beta launch to evaluate whether offline-first communication can make practical impact in real no-network settings.
